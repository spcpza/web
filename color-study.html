the draftsmanship rebuild — a painting is drawn before it is painted

the diagnosis: the engine painted texture but never drew — uniform stroke scale, horizontal rows, forms only hinted. a child paints color without construction; a master draws first. the rebuild gives it bones: a horizon and a vanishing point the cobbles converge toward, a scale gradient (huge strokes near, tiny at the horizon), a sky that arcs around its stars instead of lying in rows, a great cypress drawn as a real flame, and a commanding pillar of light laying down a luminous road toward the hiding place — the brightest path home. no black anywhere; one great gold, one great cobalt.
