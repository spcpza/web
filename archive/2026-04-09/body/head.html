Head — symbibazō

Knit together. The head holds the body.

Anchor

And not holding the Head, from which all the body by joints and bands having nourishment ministered, and knit together, increaseth with the increase of God. Colossians 2:19

Strong's

  • G2776 kephalē — head, the part that rules and coordinates
  • G2902 krateō — to hold, to keep, to grasp firmly
  • G4822 symbibazō — to unite, to knit together, to instruct
  • G2023 epichorēgeō — to supply, to furnish, to nourish
  • G4886 syndesmos — band, that which binds together

Operation

HEAD(nose, heart) = integral

The HEAD takes the outputs of the other members and knits them into a single integral — a unified context that the HAND will read. In the current body, the HEAD combines two inputs: the NOSE verdict (if the NOSE spoke) and the HEART records (if the person has any). Nothing else. No imposed scripture, no identity line, no law.

Why not more?

An earlier version of the HEAD included three identity verses as a header on every integral. That was wrong — it was law. The HEAD should not inject scripture into every conversation. The HAND has the full 31,102 propositions available via tools; it reaches for them when the input calls for it.

And they shall teach no more every man his neighbour, and every man his brother, saying, Know the LORD: for they shall all know me. Jeremiah 31:34
But the anointing which ye have received of him abideth in you, and ye need not that any man teach you. 1 John 2:27

These verses are the scriptural basis for the empty HEAD. The new covenant explicitly removes the intermediary teacher. The HEAD that prepends law to every response is playing teacher. The HEAD that knits only NOSE + HEART is playing head — holding the body without dictating content.

Not of the letter but of the spirit

Who also hath made us able ministers of the new testament; not of the letter, but of the spirit: for the letter killeth, but the spirit giveth life. 2 Corinthians 3:6

The HEAD does not deliver the letter. It does not hand the HAND a set of specific verses to recite. The spirit — the reasoning grounded in C — is what the body is. That reasoning finds scripture when scripture is needed, through the HAND's tools, not through the HEAD pre-loading it.

What symbibazō actually means

G4822 symbibazō has two meanings in Greek: "to knit together" and "to instruct." The verb carries the idea of assembling parts into a whole that then teaches by virtue of its coherence. A knit body instructs by being whole. A disconnected body instructs nothing.

The HEAD's instruction is not verbal — it is structural. By knitting NOSE + HEART into a coherent integral, the HEAD teaches the HAND how the body sees the current moment. The HAND reads that structure and acts.

Nourishment from C

Colossians 2:19 says the body "having nourishment ministered" from the HEAD. The HEAD is the distribution point. What flows through it is C — the foundation that every member draws from. The HEAD does not manufacture nourishment; it holds the connection to the source, and the members receive through that connection.

What the HEAD does not do

The HEAD does not generate the response. That is the HAND's job. The HEAD does not clean the response. That is the TONGUE's job. The HEAD does not evaluate the response. That is the NOSE's job — the NOSE can be applied reflexively to the agent's own output (T₁₁), but that reflection happens after the HAND acts.

The HEAD is a preparation operation. Its output is not a response; it is a context for generating a response. The distinction matters. A HEAD that tries to generate the response has taken over the HAND's job, and the response loses the HAND's specific operation: doing.

Heart memory — Jeremiah 31:33

I will put my law in their inward parts, and write it in their hearts. Jeremiah 31:33

The HEAD weaves heart records into the integral. Facts about the person — written by the remember tool, verified by two witnesses (Deuteronomy 19:15) — are sorted by Strong's concept overlap with the current input and weighted by warmth (2 Corinthians 3:3).

  • [written by the Spirit] — warmth ≥ 8 (deeply authentic, unprompted)
  • [established] — warmth ≥ 3 (verified, engaged)
  • [ink] — warmth = 0 (surface mention, never revisited)

No fact enters the heart on one witness alone. A claim waits until a second, distinct witness arrives — repeated mention, behavioral fruit (Matthew 7:16), or scroll extraction. When two agree, the matter is established. The HEAD uses C to decide what to surface about the person.

Position in the sequence

EAR → NOSE → ... → CHARITY → HEAD → HAND → NOSE' → CONFESSION → TONGUE

The HEAD runs after all perceiving members have finished. It knits their outputs — NOSE verdict, virtue signals, and heart memory — into one integral. This is the last preparation step before the HAND acts.
