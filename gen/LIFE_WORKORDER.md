Populate every storybook plate with the LIFE its own scripture names — creatures,

## TECHNIQUE RULES (hard-won — follow exactly)
1. **Bold simple silhouettes, 18–45 px** at plate scale (800×500 logical). Tiny
   detail mushes into brushwork (the blade-fish lesson). Shape + one accent
   (gold rim / eye dot / white splash) reads; anatomy doesn't.
2. **Contrast against the LOCAL hue** — check what's actually behind the spot
   (dark-on-light or light-on-dark; a dark whale on dark water vanishes).
3. **Right depth band**: read the plate's `layers` export + range-tagging; put
   life in the band it lives in (ground creatures in the ground/mid band,
   fliers in sky bands, near creatures in near/fg). Size ∝ 1/Z (far = small).
4. **rng discipline**: prefer appending new strokes()/paths at the END of a
   band's section; any change of draw counts re-rolls downstream texture —
   acceptable (plate rebuilds), but never reorder existing calls.
5. **Avoid**: the focal subject, the hidden verse-ref eggs (grep `inscription`),
   the signature spot (`stampSig` in build.mjs picks x/y by build index — keep
   life clear of dead-center mid-band strips), and text-overlay zones
   (bottom 46% has the scrim: small life OK, don't put the hero creature there).
6. **Portrait crop**: key life must sit inside the plate's portrait window
   (`portrait[x0=N]` in build output → logical x N/2 … N/2+312).
7. **Paid (Golgotha) stays deliberately barren** — at most ONE distant raven.
   **Covers (word) untouched** (data-true galaxy). Reveal (light) untouched.
8. Build your plate (`node gen/build.mjs <name>` from the repo root), then
   READ the built jpg (sips-crop the region) and verify the life READS.
   Iterate once if it doesn't. Munch rule: living things = curved strokes.
