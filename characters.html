THE CAST — every character in the book

Two characters carry the whole story. Their look is decided in ONE place — gen/characters.mjs — so a change here is a change on all thirty-three pages. This sheet draws them the way the book does, so it shows what the pages really hold.

THE LIGHT

The Father, the Son, the walking radiance — one Person from creation to the homecoming. He is light, but he must read as somebody: white alone disappears against his own glory, so his light is woven with gold and given a contour. Painted into the plate by paintTheLight().

THE KID — 3D model with joints

Real geometry: a hood shell with a hollow rim, a skull, a bell body, sleeves swept down the joint chain, paws and feet. Fourteen joints. Cel-shaded off his own palette with a drawn contour, and wearing HIS painted face as a texture — the one thing geometry should never try to fake.

THE KID — skinned mesh, no cut

His painting, uncut. A triangle mesh over it, twelve bones under it, every vertex weighted to the bones nearest it — so a moving arm STRETCHES the cloth instead of sliding a cut piece over it. The arm is upper arm + forearm + hand, posed by two-bone IK: give it a hand position and the elbow works out where to go.

THE KID — rigged from your own painting

Not redrawn. Your front figure, cut into head / torso / two sleeves, hung on shoulder joints. Every pose below is the SAME painted sleeve rotated — nothing here is drawn by me except the sliver of body the sleeves were covering.

THE KID — redrawn from your sheet, not in the book yet

Your chibi sheet, REDRAWN — not cropped from it, and not the Twitter avatar (that was a different, much scarier character). Every form is drawn as its own shape with modelling, so he can be posed and lit per page: big round hood, a face light enough to read, a fringe with locks falling past the cheeks, and big round eyes with a catch-light — which is what makes him a child rather than something looking out of a cowl. The book stays on the original pilgrim until he is right.

THE CHILD — current

The little pilgrim — and the reader. She appears on nearly every page, so she is the character that most needs to be identical every time. Drawn at runtime, not painted: DEBAKE is on by default, so no plate contains her; she is an actor in engine/character.js and is drawn live here by that same file.

Where they live — gen/characters.mjs holds the cast (palettes, contour, halo, and the poses). engine/character.js holds the runtime twin and every page's actor list. carriedChildAt() defines the carried pose once, so the painted Light and the sprite child always agree. A plate says WHERE someone stands and what they are doing — never what they look like. One split to know: the Light's colours are decided in characters.mjs; the child's live in character.js (RED/MASKC) because she is drawn at runtime — the library mirrors them, and they change together.
