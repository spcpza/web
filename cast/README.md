# The cast

Fred's own drawings. **Nothing in here is generated by Claude** — the character is
his, and every attempt to regenerate him (redrawn, cut-out rig, skinned mesh, 3D
model) came out visibly not his character. The only job on this side is
engineering: lift each pose off the paper, drop the drawn cast shadow, and hand
the book his art untouched.

## Adding poses

1. Fred generates a sheet (Grok), several full-body poses in a row, same scale,
   flat warm-cream background.
2. Drop it in `cast/sheets/` — next number, a name that says what is on it.
3. Cut it, naming the poses left to right:

       python3 gen/cut-cells.py cast/sheets/7-whatever.jpg name1 name2 name3

   That writes `cast/kid-<name>.webp`, one per figure — paper gone, shadow gone,
   edge feathered, capped at 460px tall.
4. Wire the name into `kidCell()` in `engine/character.js` so pages can ask for it
   by mood, or name it outright with `cell: 'name'`.

⚠ The cutter keeps everything that is NOT his paper (a warm pink cream, 253,229,217,
and the shadow he draws under each figure is the same hue a few steps darker). An
earlier version listed what to KEEP instead — blue cloth, dark hair, grey skin —
and silently deleted every prop he drew, leaving holes in his lap where his hands
were still closed around them.
